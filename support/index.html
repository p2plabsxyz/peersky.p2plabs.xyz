PeerSky Support

Need help with PeerSky on your phone, tablet or computer? Here's how to reach us, and answers to the questions we get most.

Contact us

Email peersky@p2plabs.xyz with your question. Tell us which device you use and what happened, and we'll help. We read every message.

  • Found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub for PeerSky for iPhone, iPad and Android or PeerSky Desktop.
  • Want to talk with us and other people who use PeerSky? Find us in PeerChat or on Matrix.

Report abuse

In PeerChat, touch and hold a message and choose Report. It writes an email to contact@p2plabs.xyz with what we need to act on it. Block hides everything that person sends, right away. Copyright notices go to the same address.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. PeerSky has no accounts, no passwords and no sign in, and it collects nothing about you.

How do I move to a new phone, or keep a backup? Open Settings, then Link Device. You can move everything to another device, or save an encrypted backup.

How do I delete my data? In Settings, under P2P Data, Clear all P2P data removes your drives, chats, notes and this device's P2P identity. Delete PeerChat profile is at the bottom of PeerChat's settings, and the flame button clears your tabs and history. On a phone, uninstalling PeerSky removes everything it kept.

A P2P link won't open. A hyper:// site, a shared note or a chat comes from the people who have it, not from a server, so someone who has it needs to be online. Ask them to keep PeerSky open, then try again.

What leaves my device? The privacy statements for iPhone, iPad and Android and for desktop say exactly what. The terms of use are here.
